About BOOMI

Discover our mission to bring the majestic wonders of the stars closer to Earth.

Our Mission

At BOOMI Planetarium, our core mission is to inspire endless curiosity about the cosmos. We believe that looking up at the night sky should humble and connect us all, regardless of age or background.

Founded in 2024, our facility has rapidly grown into the premier astronomical educational center in the region, bridging the gap between rigorous astrophysics and accessible public science communication.

Our Facility

The Great Dome

A massive 25-meter tilted dome providing a true 360-degree immersive viewing area with tilted seats for perfect sightlines.

Digistar 8K Tech

Equipped with 10 synchronized laser projectors that map an uninterrupted, ultra-high-definition canvas across the entire dome.

Observation Deck

An accessible rooftop featuring four computer-guided telescopes for public viewing and astrophotography sessions.

Exploring the universe and bringing the wonders of space closer to humanity through state-of-the-art technology and expert guidance.
